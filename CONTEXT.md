# Many Hands

Many Hands is a cooperative physics game where a team shares one unstable body and succeeds through coordinated action.

## Language

**Commentary cue**:
A single short line that explains or acknowledges a meaningful change using authoritative game facts. A cue may teach, heighten tension, celebrate recovery, or mark payoff, but never invents a cause.
_Avoid_: Toast, quip, narration

**Incident**:
A bounded episode of rising physical risk that ends in either failure or recovery, such as losing balance or overloading a grip.
_Avoid_: Event, warning state

**Near-fail**:
An incident that crosses a danger threshold and then recovers without becoming a failure.
_Avoid_: Near-miss, almost-win
